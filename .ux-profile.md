# UX profile: LegalLens

Read by the VectorLab UI/UX skills before any change to layout, colour, type,
motion or focus. The design itself is in `DESIGN.md`.

## Aesthetic reference

**The homepage's world, "Case file: Kettle"** (`docs/DIRECTION.md`), applied to the audit page as an
app screen: paper and ink, colour only on findings (critical red, major amber), Bricolage Grotesque
for display and Switzer for text, square corners. Elevation is a background step and a border; only
the homepage's floating sheet and flags carry a real shadow.

**Notes:**
- **Accent:** none. Actions are ink. The olive accent (v3.2) was retired on 2026-11-05 so the audit
  page and the homepage read as one product. The user had earlier rejected cobalt and pine/mint as AI-looking.
- **Concept:** the homepage scans a sample policy; the audit page is the read sheet: the document
  with notes in the margin, a spotlight, and the missing clauses. The loupe is retired.
- **Themes:** the audit page is light and dark, with a manual toggle. The homepage is dark only.

## Accessibility target

- WCAG 2.1 AA.
- Hit targets 44×44px.
- Text contrast ≥ 4.5:1, controls and focus rings ≥ 3:1, in light and dark.

## Brand tone

Plain and direct. Sentence case. Buttons say what happens ("Audit this
policy", "Print report", "Copy wording"). Findings are described as keyword
matches ("possible issue", "missing"), never as legal judgement or a violation.

## Icons

Inline SVG, 1.5px stroke, `currentColor`. No icon library. No emoji.

## Banned

The anti-slop list, plus these defaults:
- uppercase eyebrow labels;
- meta strings joined with "·";
- "→" appended to buttons;
- big-number KPI rows;
- fade-up reveal on every section;
- count-up numbers;
- identical card grids;
- warm-clay or terracotta accents, and cream paper (the paper is the homepage's neutral `#EFEFEC`);
- monospace for small data labels;
- decorative loops.

## Notes

- **Motion on the homepage** follows `docs/DIRECTION.md`: one scroll-scrubbed scan (the signature)
  and one small marking beat in "How it works". Both have reduced-motion and no-JS versions.
  - **Performance gate:** at most 5% of frames over 20ms, and no frame over 50ms.
  - **Everything else** uses the 100/150/200/300ms tokens.
- **Button press (user-requested, from their PopButton component):**
  - **The deviation:** a squash to `scaleY(0.95)` from the base over 100ms. That is deeper
    than the motion skill's ≤ 1.02 press-scale guidance; the user asked for this effect.
  - **Limits:** transform only. The bottom-edge width snaps rather than animating.
