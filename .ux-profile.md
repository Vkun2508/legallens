# UX profile: LegalLens

Read by the VectorLab UI/UX skills before any change to layout, colour, type,
motion or focus. The design itself is in `DESIGN.md`.

## Aesthetic reference

**Notion** (from `taste-archetypes.md`): content-first reading. Warm stone /
off-white paper, soft grey body text, one muted accent, elevation by a
background step and a border rather than shadow. Motion is 150–200ms, opacity and small
translates.

**Notes:**
- **Accent:** muted olive (`#5E6B3A`, sage `#B5C08A` in dark). The user rejected cobalt and pine/mint as AI-looking.
- **Concept:** the "Loupe" layout stays: the fine print under a lens, and the annotated document.
- **Themes:** light and dark, with a manual toggle.

## Accessibility target

- WCAG 2.1 AA.
- Hit targets 44×44px.
- Text contrast ≥ 4.5:1, controls and focus rings ≥ 3:1, in light and dark.

## Brand tone

Plain and direct. Sentence case. Buttons say what happens ("Review this
agreement", "Print summary"). Flags are described as keyword matches, never as
legal judgement.

## Icons

Inline SVG, 1.5px stroke, `currentColor`. No icon library. No emoji.

## Banned

The anti-slop list, plus these defaults:
- uppercase eyebrow labels;
- meta strings joined with "·";
- "→" appended to buttons;
- big-number KPI rows;
- fade-up reveal on every section;
- count-up numbers;
- identical card grids;
- warm-clay or terracotta accents (the stone paper is the archetype's, not a cream default);
- monospace for small data labels;
- decorative loops.

## Notes

- **Motion exceptions (exactly two):**
  1. **Lens first pass:** the homepage lens may make one pass of up to 900ms when the page
     loads. Transform only, runs once, skipped under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
  2. **Landing scanner (user-requested):** on the homepage only, the fine print may loop,
     drifting up one screen every 90s. Risky phrases take their highlight as they cross the
     hero's scan line.
     - **How it moves:** transform on the compositor, plus class flips (a 150ms colour fade).
     - **When it stops:** paused when the tab is hidden; static under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
     - It is the only decorative loop allowed.
  - **Performance gate (both):** at most 5% of frames over 20ms, and no frame over 50ms.
    Whichever fails is removed, not slowed down.
  - **Everything else** uses the 100/150/200/300ms tokens.
- **Button press (user-requested, from their PopButton component):**
  - **The deviation:** a squash to `scaleY(0.95)` from the base over 100ms. That is deeper
    than the motion skill's ≤ 1.02 press-scale guidance; the user asked for this effect.
  - **Limits:** transform only. The bottom-edge width snaps rather than animating.
- **Size floor:** the homepage fine print is set at 12px, the floor, on purpose.
  It is decorative (`aria-hidden`), and the lens shows it at 24px.
