Questions

What happens to my document?
It is processed in memory on this server and discarded as soon as the results page is sent. Nothing is saved, nothing is sent to another service, and the document's content is never written to the server's logs. A web page you scan is handled the same way.
Is this legal advice?
No. LegalLens matches your wording against its rulebook. It can miss wording it doesn't expect and can't judge whether a clause is lawful, so it reports possible issues, never violations. Have a lawyer review critical findings before you rely on them.
What does "not found" mean?
No clause in your policy matched a rule that the law expects a policy to cover. It is a prompt to check, and each one comes with wording you can adapt.
Why is the score an estimate?
It weighs each rule by its severity (critical, major or minor) across the rules that apply to your kind of business. A keyword check can't confirm a clause is lawful, so the score is a guide, not a grade.
Can it read my website?
Give the address of your privacy policy, or just your homepage and LegalLens follows its privacy policy link. Pages that need JavaScript or block automated visitors can be pasted instead.
