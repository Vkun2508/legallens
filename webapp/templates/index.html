How it works

  1. § 1

    Upload

    Drop in a PDF with selectable text or a .txt file, or paste the text. It stays in memory and is discarded once your results are sent.

  2. § 2

    Segment

    Numbering, headings and labelled paragraphs split the document into clauses, each tied to its exact place in the extracted text.

  3. § 3

    Flag

    Each clause is checked against watched terms in {{ category_count }} categories and given a tier, with the matching phrases shown as evidence.

What LegalLens flags

Each category is a set of watched phrases. A clause takes the tier of the most serious category it matches. "No flags" means no watched phrase was found, not that a clause is harmless.

What happens to your document

It is processed in memory on this server and discarded as soon as the results page is sent. Nothing is saved, and the document's content is never written to the server's logs.

What the flags mean

Flags come from keyword rules, the first of the methods this project compares. They show which topics a clause touches, with the matching phrases as evidence. They can miss terms worded differently, flag harmless mentions, and never say whether a clause is fair or enforceable.
