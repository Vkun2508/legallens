How to read these results
  • Tiers come from keyword rules. A tier shows which topics a clause touches and how closely it is worth reading. It does not say whether a clause is unfair, unusual or enforceable, and nothing here is legal advice.
  • The rules can miss terms worded differently and can flag harmless mentions. Each note in the margin shows the exact phrases matched. "No flags" means no watched phrase was found, not that a clause is safe.
  • The document shown is the text extracted from the file after clean-up: page headers, footers and page numbers are removed and words hyphenated across lines are rejoined. It is not the PDF's page layout.
  • Section numbers can be missing when a PDF's font hides them. Such clauses are called "Clause" plus their position.
  • A paragraph whose last line reaches the page margin can merge with the next one. Clauses over {{ long_clause_chars }} characters are marked "long".
  • Numbered lists inside a clause, such as "1) … 2) …", may be split into separate clauses. Scanned PDFs have no text to extract (no OCR yet), and two-column layouts have not been tested.
