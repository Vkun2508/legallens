How to read these results
  • This is rule-based structure splitting only. Clauses are not checked for risk or explained, and nothing here is legal advice.
  • Offsets and the text on the right refer to the text extracted from the file after clean-up: page headers, footers and page numbers are removed and words hyphenated across lines are rejoined. It is not the PDF's page layout.
  • Section numbers can be missing when a PDF's font hides them. Such clauses show "no number".
  • A paragraph whose last line reaches the page margin can merge with the next one. Clauses over {{ long_clause_chars }} characters are marked "long".
  • A heading applies to every clause after it until the next heading, so a document title or a "Last updated" line can appear as the heading of many clauses.
  • Numbered lists inside a clause, such as "1) … 2) …", may be split into separate clauses.
  • Scanned PDFs have no text to extract (no OCR yet), and two-column layouts have not been tested.
