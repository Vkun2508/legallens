How to read these results
  • Possible issue means a clause uses wording that resembles something the law restricts. Read it: a keyword check can't tell "we sell your data" from "we never sell your data".
  • Missing means no clause matched what the law expects a policy to say. If you cover it in wording LegalLens doesn't recognise, you can ignore it.
  • The score weighs each rule by severity (critical 10, major 5, minor 2). A possible issue counts half. It is an estimate, not a grade from any regulator.
  • Each rule cites the law it rests on. "Checked against the official text" means its quote was compared with the Gazette; the dark-pattern rules have not been yet.
  • Most DPDP Act duties apply from 13 May 2027. The E-Commerce Rules (2020) and the dark-pattern guidelines (2023) apply now.
  • The document shown is the text extracted from your file after clean-up, not its page layout. Scanned PDFs have no text to read.
  • This is compliance help, not legal advice. Rulebook {{ report.rulebook_version }}.
