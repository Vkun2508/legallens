Kettle Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

1. Who we are

Kettle is a weekly meal-kit app run by Kettlebrook Labs Private Limited, 4th Floor, 18 Residency Road, Bengaluru 560025.

2. What we collect

We collect your name, email address, mobile number and delivery address to create your account and deliver your orders, and your order history to suggest recipes.

3. Your consent

By using the Kettle app, you consent to the collection, use and sharing of your personal data as described in this policy.

4. Withdrawing consent

To withdraw consent, send a signed letter by registered post to our registered office. Withdrawal takes effect within 30 days.

5. Legal basis

We also process your data for our legitimate interests, such as analytics and product research.

6. Free trial

Your first two weeks are free. After the free trial, your plan automatically converts to a paid weekly plan and your card is charged without further notice.

7. Sharing

We share your delivery address with our delivery partners only to deliver your orders.

8. Storage and security

Your data is stored on servers in India and protected with encryption and access controls.

9. Keeping your data

We may retain your personal data indefinitely to improve our services.

10. Your rights

You may ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete your data.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to our Data Protection Officer, Asha Menon, at dpo@kettle.example.
